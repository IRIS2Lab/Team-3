# Team-3

Team 3, your experiment will be to measure detection and tracking performance during various illumination conditions using different colors of coffee cups (red, green, black, white). One color of each coffee cup will be prewarmed (use hot water from restroom or microwave) to provide a thermal signature. The experiment will involve adaptive shooting of specific coffee cups based on temperature and color. You will have a nerf gun that will be co-aligned with your camera. In you code you will shoot at a specified coffee cup (color and temperature based) with a confidence of 80% or higher.
